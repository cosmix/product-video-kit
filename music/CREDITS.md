# Sample credits

The soundtrack is rendered from two free sample libraries under `music/samples/`.
Everything else (kick, claps, snaps, sub bass, synth pluck, risers, sub drop,
room and hall reverbs) is synthesized in `scorekit/synth.py` and `scorekit/mix.py`.

## Salamander Grand Piano V3 (48 kHz / 24-bit)

- Source: Alexander Holm, https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html
- Files: `samples/SalamanderGrandPianoV3_48khz24bit/` (Yamaha C5, 16 velocity layers)
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0),
  http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Used for: all piano parts (`piano`).

## VSCO 2 Community Edition

- Source: Versilian Studios, https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
- Files: `samples/VSCO-2-CE-master/`
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication),
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Used for:
  - Strings: Violin Section (Spic, Pizz, susVib), Viola Section (spic, pizz, susvib),
    Cello Section (spic, pizzT, susvib), Solo Contrabass (Spic, Pizz), Harp
  - Mallets: Marimba, Xylophone, Glockenspiel
  - Brass: French Horn (sus)
  - Percussion (VSCO 1 Percussion folder): muted orchestral bass drum, snare
    drum 2, snare 1 rim clicks, Camo's shaker, tambourine (up/down), tenor
    drums (lower/higher), large ethnic frame drum (mallet), suspended cymbal
    (soft-mallet hits and rolls), finger cymbal, wind chimes (ascending)
  - Percussion (Percussion/temp): Triangle 3

No other third-party audio is used.
