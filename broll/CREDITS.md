# Credits

B-roll generated with Gemini Omni (`tools/genvideo.py`, prompts in `gen/shots.json`) needs no
attribution and gets no section here. A CC BY clip gets a `## <clip name>` section with these
lines; only clips with a section are credited on screen and in `edit/out/CREDITS.txt`:

```markdown
## <clip name>

- Title: <title as published>
- Channel: <channel or author>
- URL: <source URL>
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)
- Range used: <start-end in the source>
```
